1North Star

What this game is. What it isn't. What ending we're designing toward.

Pitch

Von Neumann Swarm is a web-based incremental/idle game about self-replicating probes spreading through a galaxy. The player starts with one probe on one asteroid; ends — eventually — with a swarm whose drift across generations produces something qualitatively new.

Spine

Prestige-evolution lineage. Each lineage is one prestige run. During a lineage, the swarm earns Adaptations (transient traits from in-game events). At reset, the player chooses 1-2 to become Imprints — permanent traits that accumulate across all future lineages, giving the swarm a cumulative always-stronger arc. Idle-dominant pacing; meta-progression across lineages. The persistent galaxy carries memory of previous lineages.

Terminology (proposed): Adaptations (earned, transient), Imprints (chosen, permanent), Drift (the overall identity-change concept).

Touchstones (decreasing influence): Increlution · Antimatter Dimensions · Kittens Game · Universal Paperclips · Factorio.

v1 scope v1

Two prestige layers active: Generation (within a single lineage) and Lineage (drift carries forward). Persistent galaxy across lineages, with site-scale Lineage Remnants from previous lineages discoverable on the map and interactable (reclaim / salvage / negotiate / fight). Capstone designed-but-not-built; v1 ships as an open-ended prestige game with the deepest lineage tier as the visible destination.

Capstone (aspirational) v2 roadmap

Hybrid A+C: Swarm Transcendence + Cosmological Race. The cosmological clock advances across Eras; at heat death, a transcendent swarm-descendant chooses what to seed into the next universe. v1 architecture leaves explicit stubs (inactive cosmological clock field; NG+ scaffolding); v2 turns them on.

Anti-goals

  • Not Cookie Clicker shaped — single-loop with one prestige is explicitly out of scope.
  • Not a pure number-go-up game; drift and persistent galaxy carry identity, not just multipliers.
  • Not Factorio — no spatial belt-and-inserter optimization. The galaxy is a state graph, not a layout puzzle.
  • Not a narrative game — no scripted dialog; lore and consequence emerge from systems.
  • Not pay-to-progress; no monetization design in this spec.
  • Not infinite-stat-creep. Imprint accumulation must be absorbed by content tier-up + opportunity-cost in Imprint design (mutually exclusive families). The game stays interesting at lineage 20.
Decision Spine confirmed 2026-05-15 from three options (story-arc, sandbox+capstone, prestige-lineage).
Decision v1 scope confirmed 2026-05-16 over CC-style collapse and trimmed-mechanics fallback.

2Session-Shape Contract

Explicit promises about what idle costs vs. what active play rewards. The most-broken contract in incremental games.

TBD. Open question: where on the idle ↔ active spectrum does each layer sit? See Open Questions Q2.

Provisional framing

  • Generation layer: idle-dominant. Check-ins of a few minutes, several times a day, are the expected primary mode. Active sessions of 30-60 min are rewarded with meaningful decisions (build orders, expansion targets, reset timing) but not with raw throughput.
  • Lineage layer: active-dominant at transition points. Choosing when to reset, what to carry, what to seed into next lineage are all real decisions. Between resets, idle behavior takes over.
  • Offline catch-up: full credit, computed analytically (not by replaying ticks). Required, not optional, given idle-dominant pacing. See Tech Spine §5.
Review note slot — is the "active at transition points" idea right, or should active matter throughout?

4Systems

Each system gets its own sub-spec: state shape, inputs, outputs, dependencies, prestige carryover behavior.

Confirmed systems (sub-specs TBD)

  • Lineage Remnants v1 — site-scale legacy from previous lineages discoverable on the map. Interaction modes: reclaim / salvage / negotiate / fight. Encounter outcomes can seed Adaptations for current lineage. Reclaimed remnants inherit current Imprint stack (global scope). See Decision Log 2026-05-17. Sub-questions in Open Questions Q3a–Q3e.
  • Adaptations & Imprints (Drift System) v1 — Adaptations are transient traits earned in-lineage from events (including Remnant encounters); at reset, the player promotes 1-2 to Imprints (permanent across all future lineages). Apply globally including to reclaimed Remnant assets. Mutually-exclusive Imprint families provide opportunity cost. See Decision Log 2026-05-17. Sub-questions in Open Questions Q4a–Q4d.
  • Galaxy Topology v1 — ~250 systems, seed-driven procedural generation with hand-authored anchors (Core, anomaly zones, edge rim). Systems are positioned on a 2D galactic-disk plane (EVE-style projection) with a graph of jump connections overlaid — travel is constrained to connected neighbours, not free movement. Galactic hierarchy: Rim → Mid → Core, with different resource density, danger, and tier-gating per layer. Lineage 1 always starts at the Rim. Subsequent lineages can Seed deeper into explored regions. Travel time per jump is the primary idle-pacing lever. See Decision Log 2026-05-17. Sub-questions in Open Questions Q7a–Q7e.
  • Prestige UX (Reset Bundle) v1 — At reset the player makes three decisions: which 1-2 Adaptations to promote to Imprints; where in the galaxy to Seed the new lineage (depth-from-Rim is a real choice); what to name the new lineage. Named lineages persist in the world's history (Remnant attribution, decision log within the save). See Decision Log 2026-05-17.

Not yet designed

Pending decisions:
  • Resource model (single resource vs. resource web; if web, how many primaries)
  • Probe roles (one role with config vs. specialized roles)
  • Subsystem unlock structure (tech tree shape — linear, branching, web)
  • Galaxy topology (abstract graph vs. structured map vs. procedural)
  • Drift model (random modifiers vs. directed evolution vs. trait tree)
  • Persistent galaxy state model — partially answered by Lineage Remnants; rest TBD

5Tech Spine

Stack, simulation/UI split, save format, performance budget.

Stack: deferred until session-shape and UI complexity are pinned. Default recommendation pending the next design pass.

Working assumptions (challenge-ready)

  • Language: TypeScript (non-negotiable for a project this size).
  • Bundler: Vite. No build-system bikeshedding.
  • UI layer: probably a thin reactivity layer (Lit, Solid, or Alpine). React/Svelte must earn their seat — the UI here is mostly numbers and graphs, not heavily componentised. Will revisit when we know what the screens look like.
  • Simulation: headless deterministic tick simulation. UI is a view over the sim. Sim must run in a worker so the UI thread stays responsive.
  • Save format: versioned JSON with explicit migrators per version bump. Galaxy state likely outgrows localStorage; assume IndexedDB.
  • Offline catch-up: analytical integration of accumulation formulas, not per-tick simulation. Hard requirement — a multi-hour idle session that triggers a multi-second hang on resume is a game-killer.
  • Architecture stubs for v2: save format reserves fields for cosmological clock and aeon-carryover bundle. Tick loop has empty hooks for Era transitions. This costs almost nothing now and saves a migration later.

8Risk Register

Known design and technical risks, with mitigation thinking.

#RiskTypeMitigation
R1 Layered-prestige onboarding. Even two layers (Generation + Lineage) is enough to lose players. AD-style "you don't see the layer until you've earned it" is well-trodden but easy to get wrong. design Lineage layer should be invisible in the UI until first reset is unavoidable. Hide drift and persistent-galaxy mechanics behind the first prestige.
R2 Capstone promise vs. v1 ship. Players hearing "Von Neumann Swarm has a designed ending" and getting v1's prestige-treadmill will feel cheated. design Don't market the capstone for v1. v1 messaging: "open-ended prestige; designed capstone is a planned expansion." Be explicit in store copy.
R3 Persistent-galaxy save format. If galaxy state grows unboundedly, saves grow unboundedly. IndexedDB has limits and compression has perf cost. tech Cap on systems; LRU eviction for fully depleted/abandoned systems back to "lossy summary." Architecture must allow this from day one.
R4 Drift as random-stat-shuffle. If drift modifiers feel like a slot machine rather than meaningful identity changes to your swarm, the central distinctive mechanic loses its weight. design Drift must be readable and consequential. Player should be able to point at their swarm and say "this lineage is good at X, bad at Y, weird about Z." Trait categories rather than flat number bonuses.
R5 Idle-pacing vs. designed-destination tension. Even at v1 scope, "reach deepest lineage tier" must feel like a real destination, not infinite grind dressed up. design Lineage tier count must be finite and small enough to feel reachable (probably < 20). Each tier should be qualitatively different, not just larger numbers.
R6 Offline catch-up correctness. Analytical integration of compound systems is hard. A bug here either gives players too much (econ-breaking) or too little (rage-inducing). tech Every accumulator system must come with both a tick rule and a closed-form for arbitrary Δt, validated by property test (running closed-form vs. simulated ticks should agree to a small tolerance).
R7 Imprint stack going stale. Permanent accumulation without curation leads to "I have all the good ones; choices are now trivial." Especially acute around lineages 15-25 if the Imprint pool isn't expanding faster than the player consumes it. design Mutually-exclusive Imprint families enforce identity choices early. Pool growth tied to galactic exploration progress (new Adaptations unlock as you reach new galactic regions / Remnant types). v2 swap system is the long-term curation tool but v1 must stand on its own.
R8 Imprint promotion as the only meaningful reset choice. If the prestige moment reduces to "pick 1-2 from a list", we've made one of the most important moments in the game a flat menu. UP and AD both give prestige moments more weight than that. design Mitigated by Q5b resolution: prestige bundles Imprint promotion + Seeding choice + Lineage Name. Three decision surfaces at the reset moment instead of one.
R9 Travel-time tuning is THE balance problem. Too short and the game has no idle pacing — players micromanage every probe. Too long and the game is unplayable without 8h+ check-ins. This is the single highest-leverage balance variable in the design. design Travel time must scale with probe tech tier (early game = slow, late game = fast) AND with galactic layer (Rim slow but safe, Core fast but dangerous). Build telemetry from day one: time-to-arrival distributions, idle-window distributions, active-session durations. Tune against real play data, not theory.
R10 Connection-graph generator pathologies. A procedural graph generator that produces disconnected components, dead-end peninsulas, or impassable choke-zones will create unplayable saves. Players will discover edge cases we don't. tech Generator must guarantee: full connectivity (no orphan components); minimum degree 2 for most systems (no dead-ends except deliberate ones); a viable Rim→Core path even after damage / Remnant blockage. Property test the generator across many seeds before shipping.
